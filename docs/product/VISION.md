# Product Vision

## Problem

Cryptoasset histories are fragmented across exchanges, CSV formats and blockchains. Users and professionals spend time resolving duplicates, internal transfers, missing acquisition costs and historical price inconsistencies before accounting or tax work can begin.

## Vision

Make crypto transaction histories understandable, reconcilable and auditable without taking custody of assets.

## Initial user outcome

A user submits supported, read-only transaction data and receives a traceable diagnostic report identifying structural inconsistencies and the records that require attention.

## Initial scope

- CSV schema validation;
- canonical transaction normalization;
- duplicate and missing-field detection;
- internal-transfer classification;
- historical BRL price enrichment;
- traceable diagnostic report.

## Explicit non-goals

- custody, wallet signing or seed handling;
- trading or investment recommendations;
- automatic tax filing;
- legal or accounting representation;
- real-money payment processing during discovery.

## Product principle

The durable asset is the canonical ledger and its traceability. Interfaces, reports and jurisdiction-specific exports are adapters around that core.

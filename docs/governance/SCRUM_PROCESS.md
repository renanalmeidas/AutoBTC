# Scrum and Delivery Process

## Cadence

- One-week discovery or implementation sprints.
- Planning: select measurable outcomes and a small backlog.
- Daily asynchronous update: completed, next, blocked, evidence.
- Review: demonstrate behavior and metrics.
- Retrospective: keep one improvement with an owner.

## Definition of Ready

A story has a problem, target user, measurable result, business rules, acceptance criteria, dependencies, risk notes and owner.

## Definition of Done

Acceptance criteria pass; tests, documentation, security review and analytics are complete; no unresolved compliance or budget gate remains.

## Required flow

`Hypothesis → Product decision → Business rule → Story → Tech spec/ADR → Implementation → Review → QA/Security → Measurement`

No technical story may silently introduce a new business rule.

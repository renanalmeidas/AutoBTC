# Paid Campaign Process

## Policy

Agents may prepare and review campaigns. The owner alone enters payment details, activates campaigns and changes platform limits.

## Required proposal fields

- campaign ID and hypothesis;
- channel, audience, creative variants and landing page;
- exact total and daily maximum in BRL;
- start/end dates;
- tracking readiness;
- success, failure and stop-loss thresholds;
- legal/brand review;
- rollback or pause instructions.

## Gate sequence

1. Paid Traffic proposes.
2. Analytics validates measurement and sample size.
3. Brand/Creative validates message.
4. Compliance validates claims and audience.
5. Budget Controller verifies the machine-readable policy and remaining amount.
6. Owner explicitly approves the exact campaign.
7. Owner manually pays and activates.

The approval expires after the policy window. Platform-side changes to budget, dates, targeting or creative require a new approval.
